# Data licence

`items_main.csv` and the files in `runs/` contain patient notes, parameter values, reference answers and
tolerance intervals taken from **MedCalc-Bench-Verified**
(https://github.com/nikhilk7153/MedCalc-Bench-Verified, commit 801592132bfd833f049b517a4e013e00a5fc40fa),
derived from MedCalc-Bench (Khandekar et al., NeurIPS 2024, Datasets and Benchmarks Track).

The source dataset is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence
(CC BY-SA 4.0). These files, including the model responses to prompts that contain those notes, are
distributed under the same licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: selection of 240 examples of six calculators, unit conversion, model responses and derived tables.
